Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · App: LunaCycle (com.lunacycle.app)

Zero-Account, Local-First Summary: LunaCycle requires no sign-up, collects zero analytics or advertising identifiers, and encrypts your entire personal health log on your own device from Day 1 using 256-bit AES-GCM. Your private cycle notes, Basal Body Temperature (BBT), ovulation strip results, and intimacy logs never leave your device unless you personally export a file.

1. Categories of Personal Data Stored On-Device

When you voluntarily log entries inside LunaCycle, the following data is stored locally inside your device’s private IndexedDB vault, encrypted at rest from Day 1 with a device-local 256-bit AES-GCM key (and double-encrypted with PBKDF2-SHA256 when you enable a 4-digit PIN):

  • Cycle & Gestational Dates: Menstrual bleeding dates, flow levels, estimated due dates / LMP dates (in Pregnancy Mode), and daily pill pack reminder checkmarks.
  • Physical & Wellness Cues: Self-logged symptoms, moods, morning BBT readings, cervical mucus observations, LH strip results, sleep hours, hydration, sanitary timer changes, and private notes.
  • App Preferences: Theme colors, language selection (English, Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu), Disguise Mode, and local reminder times.

2. Zero Third-Party Trackers, Analytics, or Data Sales

  • Zero Analytics / Telemetry SDKs: No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Meta Pixel, Crashlytics, or third-party tracking scripts are embedded.
  • Zero Advertising IDs: LunaCycle never accesses Android Advertising ID (AAID) or Apple IDFA/IDFV and displays zero advertisements.
  • 100% Local Native Bundle: Installed Android and iOS builds run entirely from signed local assets in Airplane Mode without contacting external CDNs on launch.
  • OS Backup & Screenshot Protection: Android cloud auto-backup (allowBackup="false") and Recent-Apps switcher screenshots (FLAG_SECURE / iOS privacy blur) are restricted to protect sensitive screens.

3. Optional End-to-End Encrypted Partner Sync (18+ Only)

By default, LunaCycle makes zero network calls. Network transmission occurs only if an adult user (aged 18+) explicitly enables Share My Cycle in the Partner tab:

  • End-to-End Encryption (E2EE): Before leaving your phone, a privacy-scoped summary snapshot (excluding private notes, BBT decimals, LH tests, and intimacy logs) is encrypted on-device using AES-256-GCM derived from a cryptographically random 128-bit Pairing Key (shared only via URL #hash fragment or in-person QR scan, never sent to the server) plus an optional 4-digit PIN.
  • Processor & Automatic Expiry: Ciphertext envelopes are relayed through Cloudflare Workers KV (lunacycle-partner-sync) solely to deliver updates to your invited partner. Snapshots automatically expire after 30 days, and encrypted Care Pings expire after 7 days.
  • Instant Withdrawal: Tapping Stop Sharing & Wipe Relay Copy or Rotate Key immediately deletes the remote ciphertext from Cloudflare KV.

4. Device Permissions

  • Local Notifications (Optional): Used strictly on-device to schedule private reminders. Notification channels default to VISIBILITY_PRIVATE with generic non-medical text on locked screens unless you explicitly opt in to detailed lock-screen text.
  • Biometric Unlock (Optional — Face ID / Touch ID / Fingerprint): Used solely via OS hardware Keychain / Keystore APIs to unlock your local PIN vault; biometric templates are never accessed by or stored within LunaCycle.

5. Children & Under-18 Protection (DPDP Act Section 9)

LunaCycle does not knowingly collect, transmit, or process personal data of individuals under 18 years of age on any server. Cloud Partner Sync requires explicit 18+ confirmation and is completely disabled in Simple / Teen Mode, keeping all teen wellness logs 100% offline on the device. We perform zero behavioral monitoring or targeted advertising.

6. Your Rights Under India DPDP Act 2023, DPDP Rules 2025 & GDPR

As a Data Principal, you have immediate, self-service control inside Settings → Backup, Export & Data Rights:

  • Right to Access & Data Portability: Export your complete records anytime as an AES-256-GCM encrypted JSON backup, CSV spreadsheet, or printable Clinician PDF.
  • Right to Correction: Edit or clear any historical daily log directly on the Calendar or Today screen.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent & 1-Tap Erasure: Tap Erase All Data in Settings to instantaneously and irreversibly wipe your local IndexedDB vault, cancel all scheduled OS notifications, clear biometric credentials, and send an immediate DELETE wipe command to the Partner Sync relay.
  • Right to Nominate (DPDP Act Section 14): Because your records reside strictly on your own device without central accounts, you may exercise nomination directly by sharing an encrypted JSON backup and passphrase with a trusted nominee of your choice.
  • Regulatory Escalation: Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI) (or your local EEA/UK supervisory authority under GDPR) if a privacy concern remains unresolved.
